Virtual Design

Furnished & styled, from anywhere

A furniture and styling service that works wherever you live. You send us the measurements, photos, and video of your home; we meet twice on Zoom and send back concepts, boards, and a full shopping list.

What It Covers

Furniture & styling only

Virtual Design is for the pieces that go into a room — furniture and the styling that finishes it — chosen to fit your measurements and your budget.

It doesn’t include remodeling, construction drawings, or architecture. For those, see our full services.

Concepts & boards · Furniture selections · Styling · Two Zoom calls · Full shopping list · From $900

How It Works

Two Zoom calls, one list

01

Before we talk

Send us your home

Detailed measurements, photos, and video of the house come to us in advance, so the first call starts with us already knowing the space.

02

30-minute Zoom

Kickoff & discovery

We get familiar with you and the home, hear how you live in it and what you want it to feel like, talk through your budget, and explain how the process runs from here.

03

In our studio

Concepts & boards

We develop the design concept and build boards for your rooms, then select the furniture and styling pieces to fit your measurements and budget.

04

60-minute Zoom

Selections review

We present the concepts, boards, and selections, walk you through them piece by piece, and take your reactions and requests.

05

Within 7 business days

Adjustments & your list

We revise the selections from your feedback and send the full shopping list within seven business days of the review.

What You Receive

More than a list of links

Every Virtual Design package includes the thinking behind the selections, not only the selections.

Design concept

The direction for your home — the look and feel everything else is chosen to support.

Boards

Boards for your rooms, so you can see the pieces together before anything is bought.

Selections

Furniture and styling pieces chosen for your measurements and your budget, reviewed with you on Zoom.

Full shopping list

Every item in the final design in one list, revised from your feedback.

Required in Advance

What we need from you

Because we never see the house in person, the design is only as good as what you send. All three are required before the kickoff Zoom.

Detailed measurements

Accurate dimensions of the house and each room we’re furnishing, so every piece we select fits.

Photos

Clear photos of each room, including anything that’s staying in place.

Video

A walk-through video, so we can see how the rooms connect and how the light moves through them.

Packages & Pricing

Two ways to work with us

The design process is the same either way. The difference is who places the orders.

Design only

You purchase it yourself

From $900

Your shopping list is built from our wider network of vendors, so every item on it is one you can order on your own, on your own schedule.

Request a Quote

Design + procurement

We order it for you

From $1,750

We place the orders through our trade-only vendors and pass our trade discount along to you wherever we can. With some vendors that discount is much larger, thanks to curated relationships and trade-sourced connections.

  1. We send you a full list with final pricing for every item.
  2. You pay the invoice in full.
  3. We place the order for every item within 24 hours.
  4. We coordinate with a warehouser and installer local to you, or have everything shipped directly to you for install.

In the greater Seattle area, we can provide the install ourselves.
