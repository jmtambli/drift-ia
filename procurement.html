Furniture Procurement

Selected, ordered & installed

Full furniture procurement and installation across the greater Seattle area — available with or without a Drift design package.

Full Service

One team, from the plan to install day

Furnishing a home means dozens of orders, vendors, delivery windows, and boxes. We take all of it on — choosing the pieces, managing the budget, placing and tracking every order, receiving it at our warehouse, and installing it in your home.

Greater Seattle area · 5,000 SF warehouse · Our own trucks & crew · With or without design

What’s Included

Plans & themes

A furnishing plan and a design theme for the home, so every room reads as part of one idea.

Furniture selections

Every piece chosen for the space, sourced through our trade-only vendors.

Budget management

We set the budget with you and manage the selections and orders against it.

Ordering

Tracking
