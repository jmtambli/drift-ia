Airbnb & Short-Term Rental Setup

From empty house to ready to list

A full-service setup for Airbnbs and short-term rentals. We set the budget, design the property, buy and install everything, style it, and coordinate the photos — and hand it back ready to list and market.

Start a Rental Project

The Service

One team for the whole setup

Setting up a rental is a project in itself: a budget to hold, a few hundred items to choose and order, deliveries to receive, and a house to assemble, style, and photograph before the first guest can book. We take the whole thing on, start to finish, so you get one point of contact and one finished property.

Budget · Design · Procurement · Install · Styling · Photo coordination

What’s Included

Everything it takes to open

Full budget

A complete setup budget for the property, set with you up front and managed through every selection and order.

Design & theme

A plan and a theme for the whole rental, so it stands out in a feed and reads as one place in person.

Procurement

We choose, order, and track every piece through our trade-only vendors, and receive it all at our own warehouse.

Install

Our own crew and trucks deliver everything, put every piece in place, and haul away the packaging.

Styling

The finishing layer — art, bedding, accessories, and the details guests notice and photograph.

Photo coordination

We coordinate the listing photography so the property is shot styled, finished, and at its best.

The Result

A full package, ready to list and market

At the end you have a furnished, styled, photographed property and what you need to put it on the market. You don’t assemble a bed frame, wait on a delivery, or hang a picture.
